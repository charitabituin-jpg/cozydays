Cozy Days – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Wizzy Works

Cozy Days is a personal planner, journal and budget app. Your entries stay on your own device. We don't run accounts or servers that store your content, and we don't sell or share your data.

What stays on your phone

Your name, calendar, to-dos, journal entries and photos, recipes, shopping cart, expenses, loans and credit card reminders, and photobooth pictures are stored only on your device. Cutting out objects, reading receipts and estimating food are done on your device.

Optional iCloud backup

If you turn on backup, a copy of your data is saved to your own iCloud account, so you can restore it on a new phone. It is protected by Apple and is not visible to us.

Permissions we ask for

  • Camera – to take photos of receipts, products, plates and photobooth pictures.
  • Photos – to pick pictures, and to save pictures you choose to save.
  • Notifications – for to-do, bill and loan reminders and backup reminders.

You can change these any time in iPhone Settings.

Internet use

When you browse online recipes, the app asks the free TheMealDB service for recipes and pictures. Only the search words you type or the recipe you open are sent. No account or personal details are included.

Purchases

The one-time purchase and free trial are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Children

Cozy Days is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything on your device. To remove an iCloud backup, go to iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
